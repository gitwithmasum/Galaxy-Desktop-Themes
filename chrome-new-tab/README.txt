MASUM CUSTOM NEW TAB — Chrome installation

1. Unzip Masum-Custom-New-Tab.zip into a permanent folder on your PC.
2. Open Chrome and visit chrome://extensions
3. Turn on Developer mode in the upper right corner.
4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped masum-custom-new-tab folder.
5. Open a new tab. If Chrome asks, choose Keep it / confirm the extension.

The New Tab page has no Google logo or central search box. It has 12 futuristic space scenes changing every 5 seconds, animated galaxy particles and MASUM BILLAH branding in the lower left. Scenes: Moon, Earth from orbit, Mercury, Venus, Earth as a planet, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Sun and satellite. The separate Earth scene and Earth planet image make 12 photos in total. Search normally using Chrome's address bar (Ctrl+L). Click a shortcut to open it. Hover over one and click ... to edit or remove it. Use Add shortcut to make a new one. The arrow buttons at the bottom right change scenes manually. Use the gear button to pause the slideshow, turn particles off, or choose your own background image (which temporarily replaces the slideshow). Restore space slideshow to bring the 12 scenes back.

UPDATE FROM AN OLDER VERSION: Replace the previous extracted extension folder with the new folder from this ZIP, then visit chrome://extensions and click the reload icon on Masum Custom New Tab. Existing shortcuts and custom backgrounds remain saved. If you previously chose a custom photo, use the gear button and Restore space slideshow to see the rotating scenes.

If Chrome reports another extension controls the New Tab page, disable that extension at chrome://extensions first. To restore the standard New Tab page, disable or remove Masum Custom New Tab.

This extension works offline, has no analytics, and only requests storage permission to remember your shortcuts and optional custom photo.
